## 2. Signs

| Sign | Yes or no | Quote or note |
|---|---|---|
| Money, a check, gift cards, or bank details asked before a real offer | Yes | "we will mail you a check to purchase your home office equipment". In this scam the check later bounces and you are left owing the money. |
| Buying equipment from "their vendor" | Yes | "to purchase your home office equipment from our approved vendor". Buying equipment is also a dealbreaker on your card. |
| Interview only by text or chat app | Yes | "message our hiring manager on Telegram to complete onboarding". There is no interview at all, only a chat app. |
| An offer with no interview | Yes | "No interview is needed." |
| Pay far above normal for simple work | Yes | "Data Entry Assistant position at 45 dollars per hour, 10 hours per week". That is very high for data entry (inference). |
| A free email address for a company | Yes | "careers.globalhr.team@gmail.example" is a free-mail address, not a company domain. |
| A sender whose company, title, or role does not match or changes | Yes | The sender is "Global HR Careers" but the signature says "Global HR Solutions." The note says "Dear Candidate," not your name. |
| Odd spelling or mixed alphabets in names | No | Nothing odd in the names. |
| Pressure to act today | Yes | "Please reply today". |
| A link or file to open before anything is confirmed | No | No link or file. The Telegram request is the off-platform step. |
| Asks for my address, ID, or Social Security number early | Yes, partly | "Please reply today with your full name, home address and phone number". No ID or Social Security number is asked yet. |

I cannot check whether it really came through Handshake. The line "Based on your Handshake profile you have been selected" is a claim, not proof.

## 3. Verdict: LIKELY SCAM

Two strongest reasons:
- It offers a job with "No interview is needed" and a check for you to buy equipment from "our approved vendor." Real employers do not do this.
- It comes from a free email address, wants your home address today, and moves you to Telegram.

## 4. What to do next

1. Do not click anything, reply, send your address, or message on Telegram. Do not deposit or spend a check if one comes.
2. If you want to be sure, search for "Global HR Solutions" yourself, find its real website, and contact it only through that site.
3. Report the message to Handshake (use its report option on the message or employer) and tell your campus career center so other students are warned.
