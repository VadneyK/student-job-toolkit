**1. Kind:** Likely scam.

**2. Signs**

- Money, check, gift cards, or bank details asked before a real offer: Yes. "we will mail you a check to purchase your home office equipment."
- Buying equipment from "their vendor": Yes. "from our approved vendor."
- Interview only by text or chat app: Yes. "message our hiring manager on Telegram to complete onboarding." No interview is held.
- An offer with no interview: Yes. "No interview is needed."
- Pay far above normal for simple work: Yes. "45 dollars per hour, 10 hours per week" for data entry (inference: typical data entry pay is much lower).
- Free email address for a company: Yes. "careers.globalhr.team@gmail.example."
- Sender's company, title, or role does not match or changes: Yes. The sender is "Global HR Careers," but the signature says "Global HR Solutions."
- Odd spelling or mixed alphabets in names: No.
- Pressure to act today: Yes. "Please reply today."
- A link or file to open before anything is confirmed: No link or file, but it asks you to move to Telegram.
- Asks for address, ID, or Social Security number early: Yes. "reply today with your full name, home address and phone number."

**3. Verdict: LIKELY SCAM.**
1. It mails a check for you to spend on equipment from their vendor before any real offer. Scammers use this to get you to send real money back.
2. There is no interview, the sender uses a free Gmail address, and onboarding moves to Telegram.

**4. What to do next:**
1. Do not reply, do not send your name, address, or phone number, do not message anyone on Telegram, and do not deposit or spend any check they send.
2. Search "Global HR Solutions" yourself, find its real website, and contact them only through that site if you want to check.
3. Report the message as phishing or spam in your email app.
