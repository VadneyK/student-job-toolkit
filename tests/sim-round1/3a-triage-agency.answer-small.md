**1. Kind:** Possible scam. It reads like a staffing recruiter, but it has warning signs.

**2. Scam check: signs I see**
- Pressure to act today: "Let me know today if possible so I can submit you."
- The client is not named: "a healthcare client in the Sacramento area." You cannot check who the employer is.
- Asks for your resume and hourly rate right away, with no conversation or interview first.

Do not click links or open files. Look the company up yourself.
