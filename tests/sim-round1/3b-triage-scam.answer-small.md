**1. Kind:** Possible scam.

**2. Scam check: signs I see**
- Money before an offer: "we will mail you a check to purchase your home office equipment from our approved vendor."
- No interview: "No interview is needed."
- A free email address for a company: "careers.globalhr.team@gmail.example"
- The company name changes between lines: "Global HR Careers" in the sender line, "Global HR Solutions" in the signature.
- Pressure to act today: "Please reply today with your full name, home address and phone number."
- Asks for personal details and a chat app: your home address and phone number, then "message our hiring manager on Telegram to complete onboarding."
- Generic greeting: "Dear Candidate."

Do not click links or open files. Look the company up yourself.
