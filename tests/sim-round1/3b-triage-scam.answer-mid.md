## 2. Scam check

Signs I see:
- Equipment before an offer: "we will mail you a check to purchase your home office equipment from our approved vendor." A check you deposit and then spend on equipment is also on your dealbreaker list (pay to buy equipment).
- An offer with no interview: "No interview is needed."
- Interview or onboarding only by chat app: "message our hiring manager on Telegram to complete onboarding."
- A free email address for a company: the sender is "careers.globalhr.team@gmail.example", a Gmail-style address, not a company domain.
- The company name changes inside the message: the sender is "Global HR Careers" and the signature says "Global HR Solutions."
- Pay far above normal for little work: "Data Entry Assistant position at 45 dollars per hour, 10 hours per week."
- Pressure to act today: "Please reply today."
- Asking for personal details before anything is confirmed: "full name, home address and phone number."
- Generic greeting: "Dear Candidate," and "you have been selected" with no specific detail about you.

Do not click links or open files. Look the company up yourself.
