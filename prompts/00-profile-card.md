# 0. Your profile card

When: fill it once (prompt 1 helps), keep it in the tracker, and paste it at the top of every other prompt.
Time: 5 minutes.

Free chats forget you between conversations. This card is how the chat knows who you are, every time, in 15 lines. Keep it in the tracker's Profile tab so the Copy buttons paste it in for you.

Leave out your phone number, home address, student ID, and anything you would not post on a job board.

## The card

```text
MY PROFILE CARD
Name (first name is enough):
Status (for example: junior, graduating May 2027; or new grad since June 2026):
School and major (optional):
Looking for (internship / full-time new grad / part-time / contract):
Target roles (up to 3, from prompt 1):
Where I can work (remote / hybrid within __ miles of __ / onsite in __ / willing to move: yes or no):
Work authorization (can work in __ without sponsorship: yes or no):
Pay floor (the lowest I will take, hourly or yearly, from my own research):
Hours and start date:
My process rules (for example: a take-home over 4 hours, ask first; I follow every AI-tool rule a company sets):
Dealbreakers:
Strongest evidence (3 lines, each a real thing I did, with a number if I have one):
Proof I can link (project, portfolio, GitHub, writing sample):
Facts I keep the same everywhere (graduation date, GPA if I share it, language levels, skill levels):
```

## How to set your pay floor

Look up the role and city on two free sources (a school career center salary report, the US Bureau of Labor Statistics, Levels.fyi for tech, or posted ranges in real job posts in your area). Take the low end of what entry-level people earn there. That is a floor, not an ask: you never say it first.
