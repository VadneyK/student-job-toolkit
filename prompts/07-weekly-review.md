# 7. Weekly review

When: once a week, same day. Right before your job club meets works well.
Time: 10 minutes.

A search improves only if each week learns from the last. This review separates what happened (facts) from what you think it means (guesses), and turns both into one or two changes for next week.

## Paste this into a new chat

```text
Run my weekly job-search review. Be honest and kind. Separate facts from inferences and label each. Do not cheer me up with things that are not true, and do not be harsh either.

My profile card:
[PASTE YOUR PROFILE CARD HERE]

My week (paste your row from the job club sheet, or a list: what I applied to, who I asked for help, what came back):
[PASTE YOUR WEEK HERE]

1. What happened: applications, replies, interviews, rejections, offers, with counts.
2. What came back: any reason a company gave, quoted.
3. What I learned: each line labeled Fact or Inference. Look for patterns: which role families or levels got replies, which got none, where applications went that my own card says to skip.
4. Claims check: anything I said differently in two places this week (dates, skills, levels).
5. Next week, at most 3 changes. Keep applying: every role that passes the fit check gets a short note naming one real detail from the post. Add one proof-of-work step if I have a role I really want.
6. One kind of person I could ask for advice this week (a field, not a name), and what to ask them. Prompt 4 will write the message.
7. One line for my job club sheet: my goal for next week.
```

## Notes

- More applications raise your chances, so do not cut back on roles you fit. What to cut is applications to roles you would fail on level, eligibility, or papers: they cost time and bring nothing back.
- Keep each week's review. Paste last week's "next week" list in with this week's so the chat can check whether you did it.
