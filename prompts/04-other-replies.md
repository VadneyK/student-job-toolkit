# 4. The other replies: follow up, thank you, ask for feedback, they answered

When: after the first message. Pick the mode.
Time: 2 minutes.

## Paste this into a new chat

```text
Help me write one short job-search message. Use only the facts I give. Under 90 words, plain and warm, signed with my first name. No buzzwords, no flattery, no claims that are not on my card.

My profile card:
[PASTE YOUR PROFILE CARD HERE]

Mode (keep one, delete the rest):
- FOLLOW UP: I applied or they went quiet. One polite nudge, one new fact or question, no guilt.
- THANK YOU: after an interview. Thank them, name one specific thing we talked about, say what I'd bring.
- ASK FOR FEEDBACK: after a no. Thank them, ask one specific question ("Was there one thing that would have made my application stronger?"), keep the door open.
- THEY ANSWERED: they replied to my questions. Rescore the 5 checks (role fit, level, pay, place and papers, process) from their answers, then: all Pass, write a yes-let's-talk reply with [TIME 1], [TIME 2], [TIME 3]; any Fail, write a kind no thanks; they skipped the pay question, do not ask again and tell me so I can decide.
- NUMBER FIRST: they asked my pay number before giving a range. Write a reply that asks for the range for the level first, kindly.

The thread so far (paste it):
[PASTE THE THREAD HERE]

Then give me one line for my tracker: the next step and a date.
```

## Timing that works

- Follow up once after 7 days of silence, then once more after 14. Then let it go and log it as closed.
- Thank-you notes go the same day.
- Ask for feedback only once, and only where a person (not a no-reply address) sent the no.
