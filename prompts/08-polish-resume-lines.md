# 8. Polish your resume lines (optional)

When: after the kit page's Resume tab has turned your experience log into lines, and you want them tighter.
Time: 3 minutes.

The kit page writes your lines from plain templates, with no AI, so they are already true. This prompt only tightens the wording. It must keep every fact and add none. If you are aiming at one job, paste the post too and it will pick the lines that matter most for it.

Church and community service counts. Leading a small group, running an event booth, tutoring, or managing a sign-up sheet are real experience. Describe them as what they were, name the group honestly, and let the actions and numbers do the work.

## Paste this into a new chat

```text
Tighten these resume lines for a student or new grad. Rules: keep every fact; add no facts, tools, numbers, or results I did not write. Keep the word "About" wherever a number is an estimate. Each line starts with a strong past-tense verb (present tense if the entry ends in "now") and stays under 20 words. Name the group as I named it; do not hide or rename it.

My profile card:
[PASTE YOUR PROFILE CARD HERE]

My lines (from my experience log):
[PASTE YOUR EXPERIENCE LINES HERE]

A job post I am aiming at (optional; write NONE if there is none):
[PASTE THE JOB POST HERE, OR WRITE NONE]

1. Rewrite each line. Show the old line and the new line.
2. Mark any line you could not improve without inventing something, and give me one question to ask myself that would make it stronger (for example: "How many people came?").
3. If I gave a job post: pick the 3 lines that matter most for it, quoting the line of the post each one answers.
4. List any line that sounds bigger than the facts behind it, and the honest version.
```

## If it goes wrong

- It adds a tool or number you did not write: reply "Remove anything I did not write myself."
- It drops "About" from an estimate: reply "Keep About on estimated numbers."
