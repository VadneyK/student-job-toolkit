# 7. The daily 10 minutes

When: once a day, same time. Morning works for most people.
Time: 10 minutes.

You do not need an assistant reading your inbox. You need the same small routine every day:

1. **Open the tracker.** The Today list shows every step that is due. Do them, or tap Snooze.
2. **Check mail and messages** (email, LinkedIn, Handshake) for anything about a job. Skip alerts and newsletters.
3. **Paste the new ones** into the prompt below, up to 5 at once.
4. **Log each one** in the tracker with its next step and a date.
5. **Pick one thing** that moves the search forward today: one careful application, one reply, or one hour on a proof-of-work project.

## Paste this into a new chat

```text
Sort today's job messages. Use only what is in them and my card. Never guess a Pass.

My profile card:
[PASTE YOUR PROFILE CARD HERE]

Today's messages (up to 5; separate them with ---):
[PASTE THE MESSAGES HERE]

For each message, one block:
- From and kind: real person about a role / alert or newsletter / scheduling / rejection / offer / possible scam.
- Scam signs, if any. If any: "Do not click links. Look the company up yourself."
- For a role: role fit, level, pay, place and papers, process, each Pass, Fail, or Unknown.
- Reply to send: A questions first, B yes let's talk, C kind no thanks, or none.
- Tracker line: next step and date.

Then: the one thing I should do first today, in one line.
Do not write the replies yet. I will ask for each one with "write reply 1", "write reply 2", and so on, using the reply rules: under 90 words, at most 3 questions, pay first, never state my number first.
```

## Notes

- Keep the routine even on slow days. Most days it takes 3 minutes.
- Do not reply at midnight. Write the reply, then send it in the morning.
