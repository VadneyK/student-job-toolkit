# 2. Honest fit check on a job post

When: before you spend more than 2 minutes on an application, and before you ask anyone to refer you.
Time: 2 minutes per post.

Five checks that trip students up most, each Pass, Fail, or Unknown, scored only from the post and your card. Unknown is allowed; a guessed Pass is not. It ends in one verdict: apply, skip, or stop. Skip the roles you cannot get, then put real effort into the rest. A helper can refer you with a clear conscience only to a role you actually fit.

## Paste this into a new chat

```text
Act as a careful, honest fit checker for a student or new grad. Use only my profile card and the job post below. Never mark Pass on a guess; use Unknown. For each check, quote the line of the post you used.

My profile card:
[PASTE YOUR PROFILE CARD HERE]

The job post (the text, not a link):
[PASTE THE JOB POST HERE]

Step 0. Is it real? List any scam sign: money, equipment, or bank details asked before an offer; interview only by chat app; an offer with no interview; pay far above normal for little work; a free email address for a company; company facts that change or cannot be found. If you see one, write STOP with the reason and skip the rest.

Step 1. Is this one of the target roles on my card? Answer yes, stretch, or no, with one line of evidence from my card.

Step 2. Score 5 checks as Pass, Fail, or Unknown:
1. Graduation window and eligibility: my graduation date fits the start date or class year they want, and I meet any degree, major, GPA, or "currently enrolled" rule.
2. Work authorization: what they require or sponsor fits what my card says.
3. Level: Pass for internship, new grad, entry level, or no years stated. Watch the trap: a post titled "entry level" that asks for "3+ years required" fails. "Preferred" years alone do not fail. Fail for a Senior title or required years I do not have.
4. Location: the place and the days onsite fit my card.
5. Process: the interview steps fit my process rules (take-home length, AI-tool policy). Unknown if not stated.

Step 3. Pay: if the post gives a range, say whether its top reaches my floor. If not, list it as a question.

Step 4. Verdict, one of:
APPLY: no Fail, role is yes or stretch. List each Unknown, and pay if unknown, as questions for my first conversation.
SKIP: any Fail, or role is no. Name the first reason in one line.
STOP: a scam sign from step 0.

Step 5. If APPLY: write a 3-line note for the application that names one real detail from this post and one real thing from my card. No buzzwords. Nothing that is not on my card.

Step 6. One honest sentence on my chances, and the one thing that would raise them most.

Format: a table for step 2 (check, result, quoted line), then short lines.
```

## What you should get back

A role answer, a 5-row table, a pay line, a verdict, and for APPLY a 3-line note. Keep the verdict: prompt 4 (asking a member in your field) uses it.

## If it goes wrong

- It marks a check Pass that the post never mentions: reply "That is not in the post. Mark it Unknown."
- It says SKIP for "2 years preferred": reply "Preferred is not required. Rescore the level check."
- It misses "3+ years required" under an "entry level" title: reply "Read the requirements again for required years."
- The note sounds like a robot: reply "Shorter. Use my words from the card."
