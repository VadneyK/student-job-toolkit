# 2. Check a job post (the match test)

When: before you spend more than 2 minutes on any application.
Time: 2 minutes per post.

Five checks, each Pass, Fail, or Unknown, scored only from the post and your card. Unknown is allowed; a guessed Pass is not. It ends in one verdict: apply, skip, or stop because it looks like a scam. A few careful applications beat a hundred quick ones.

## Paste this into a new chat

```text
Act as a careful, honest job-match checker. Use only my profile card and the job post below. Never mark Pass on a guess; use Unknown. For each check, quote the line of the post you used.

My profile card:
[PASTE YOUR PROFILE CARD HERE]

The job post (the text, not a link):
[PASTE THE JOB POST HERE]

Step 0. Is it real? List any scam sign: money, equipment, or bank details asked before an offer; interview only by chat app; an offer with no interview; pay far above normal for little work; a free email address for a company; company facts that change or cannot be found. If you see one, write STOP with the reason and skip the rest.

Step 1. Score 5 checks as Pass, Fail, or Unknown:
1. Role fit: the work matches a target role on my card, with evidence in my card.
2. Level: Pass for internship, new grad, entry level, or no years stated. "Preferred" years alone do not fail. Fail for a Senior or Staff title, or "required" years I do not have.
3. Pay: Pass if the top of the range reaches my floor. Fail if the whole range is below it. Unknown if not stated.
4. Place and papers: the location fits my card, and the work authorization or sponsorship fits mine.
5. Process: the steps fit my process rules. Unknown if not stated.

Step 2. Verdict, one of:
APPLY: no Fail. List each Unknown as a question for my first conversation.
SKIP: any Fail. Name the first one in one line.
STOP: a scam sign from step 0.

Step 3. If APPLY: write a 3-line note for the application that names one real detail from this post and one real thing from my card. No buzzwords. Nothing that is not on my card.

Step 4. One honest sentence on my chances, and the one thing that would raise them most.

Format: a table for step 1 (check, result, quoted line), then short lines.
```

## What you should get back

A 5-row table, a verdict, and for APPLY a 3-line note. Add the job to the tracker with that verdict.

## If it goes wrong

- It marks Pay as Pass when the post has no pay: reply "Pay is not in the post. Mark it Unknown."
- It says SKIP for "2 years preferred": reply "Preferred is not required. Rescore check 2."
- The note sounds like a robot: reply "Shorter. Use my words from the card."
