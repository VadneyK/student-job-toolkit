# The guide, in plain words

This kit is for college students and new grads looking for an internship or a first job. It costs nothing. It works with a free Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini account. You need no API key, no paid plan, and no app to install.

It does not apply for you, and it will not make anything up for you. It helps you do four things well: know what you can honestly go after, spend time only on jobs that fit, answer recruiters the right way, and show your work.

## Start in 10 minutes

1. **Open the tracker.** Download `tracker/index.html` and open it in your phone or laptop browser. It works offline. Bookmark it.
2. **Make your profile card (5 minutes).** In the tracker, tap **Prompts**, then **Copy with my card** on prompt 1. Open a new free chat, paste, and replace `[PASTE YOUR BACKGROUND HERE]` with your resume text, classes, projects, and jobs. Copy the filled card it gives back into the **Card** tab. Fix anything that is not true, and answer every ASK ME yourself.
3. **Check one real job post (2 minutes).** Find a post you are thinking about. Tap **Copy with my card** on prompt 2, paste it into a new chat, and paste the post's text where it says. You get 5 checks and a verdict: apply, skip, or stop.
4. **Log it.** Tap **Jobs**, add the job with its verdict and a next step with a date.

That is the whole setup. From then on, it is 10 minutes a day and 10 minutes a week.

## Every day (10 minutes)

Prompt 7 has the routine. Open the tracker's **Today** tab, do what is due, check your messages, paste the new ones into prompt 7, log each one, and pick one thing that moves you forward.

## Every week (10 minutes)

On the **Backup** tab, tap **Copy weekly summary**, then paste it into prompt 6. It tells you what happened, what it likely means (labeled as a guess), and at most 3 changes for next week. Download a backup while you are there.

## The ideas behind it

**Fit beats volume.** Sending 100 quick applications feels productive and mostly teaches you nothing. Five careful ones a week, each with a short note that names one real detail from the post, teach you what works. The match test is how you pick the five.

**Unknown is an honest answer.** The match test never guesses. If a post does not list pay, pay is Unknown, and you find out before you spend an evening on it.

**Ask before you book.** When a recruiter writes, you ask the few questions that decide fit (pay, place, process) before you give up an hour. You do not name your pay number first. If a staffing agency writes, you ask them not to submit your profile until you have talked.

**Check for scams first.** Real employers do not ask for money, bank details, or equipment purchases before an offer, and do not interview only over a chat app. Prompts 2, 3, and 7 check for these signs before anything else. If one shows up, do not click links or open files; look the company up yourself.

**Show, do not just tell.** For a role you really want, build a small piece of the actual work from public information, write down every decision, and list the questions only the company could answer (prompt 5). It is often the thing that gets a person to read your application.

**Tell the truth.** Every prompt tells the chat to use only what you gave it and to label guesses. Keep your facts the same everywhere: dates, GPA, language levels, skill levels. If a company says no AI tools on a take-home or a test, follow that. A gap you are honest about costs you less than a claim that falls apart in an interview.

## Free plan tips

- Start a new chat for each prompt. Long chats on free plans get slow and forget the start.
- If the answer stops halfway, type `continue`.
- If you hit the free limit, come back later or switch to another free chat. The tracker keeps your place.
- Paste text, not links. Free chats often cannot open links, and many job pages block them anyway.
- Your card holds no phone, address, or ID numbers, so it is safe to paste. Most free chats let you turn off training on your chats in settings if you prefer.

## Your data

The tracker stores everything in your browser on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Browsers can clear saved pages (private windows, clearing site data, some phones), so download a backup each week from the **Backup** tab. To move to a new device, open the tracker there and restore the backup file.
