# The job circle: doing this with friends

A job search alone is slow and discouraging. A small circle of friends who meet once a week makes it steadier, and it turns every lead that does not fit you into one that might fit someone else.

## The shape

- 3 to 5 people. Same school or not, same field or not.
- 30 minutes, once a week, same time. A video call or a table in the library.
- Everyone uses the same kit, so everyone speaks the same language: match test, Unknown, apply, skip.

## The 30 minutes

1. **Round (2 minutes each).** Each person reads their weekly summary from the tracker (Backup tab, Copy weekly summary). Counts and stages only. Nobody has to share notes, pay numbers, or rejections they would rather keep.
2. **One post together (10 minutes).** One person brings a post they are unsure about. Everyone reads it and calls each of the 5 checks out loud. Then compare with what the chat said. People disagree most on level and role fit, and that is where you learn.
3. **Leads (5 minutes).** Share posts that did not fit you but might fit someone here. In the tracker, tick "A friend could use this lead" on those jobs, then Backup tab, Copy leads as text, and paste it in your group chat. Friends paste it into their tracker's Backup tab under "Restore or add a friend's leads". Only company, role, and link travel; never your notes.
4. **Commit (3 minutes).** Each person says out loud their plan for next week: how many careful applications (5 is a good number), and one proof-of-work step if they have a role they really want.

## Ground rules

- Celebrate replies and interviews, not application counts.
- What is said in the circle stays in the circle.
- Be honest and kind with each other's match tests, the same way the prompts are with you.
- A referral is a real favor. Only refer a friend for a role their match test passes, and tell them what you know about the team.

## For the person who starts the circle

Send your friends the tracker file and the guide, and pick a time. That is all the setup there is. The first meeting can just be everyone doing the 10-minute start from the guide together.
