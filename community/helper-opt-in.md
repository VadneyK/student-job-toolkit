# Helper opt-in form

Copy this into a free form tool (Google Forms, Microsoft Forms) owned by your community's account, not a personal one. Only the coordinators can see the answers. Responses go to one private sheet.

## Form text

**Title:** Help a student in your field

**Description:**
Students in our community are looking for internships and first jobs. The thing that helps them most is 20 minutes with someone who does the work they want. If you are willing, tell us your field and what you can offer. A coordinator will match you with at most the number of students you choose, and introduce you by email. Only the coordinators see this form. You can change your answers or withdraw any time by emailing [COORDINATOR EMAIL].

**Questions:**

1. Your name (required)
2. Email the coordinator can use to introduce you (required)
3. Your field or industry (required, short answer. For example: "software engineering", "nursing", "public accounting", "high school teaching")
4. Your job title (optional)
5. Your employer (optional. Leave blank if you would rather not say. It helps us match referral asks, which are always your choice.)
6. City, or remote (optional)
7. What you can offer (required, check all that apply):
   - A 20-minute call about the field
   - A look at a resume, with written comments
   - A practice interview (30 to 45 minutes)
   - A referral at my company, only when the student is a real fit and I am comfortable
8. How many students a month, at most? (required: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 or more)
9. When are you usually free? (check all: weekday evenings / weekends)
10. Are you an alum of one of our campus groups? Which one? (optional. Students like talking to someone who walked the same path.)
11. Anything the coordinator should know (optional. For example: "only students in their last year", "happy to help with grad school applications too")
12. Confirmation (required checkbox): "I understand only the coordinators will see this, that I will be introduced by email at most [answer 8] times a month, that I never owe anyone a referral, and that I can withdraw any time."

## What the coordinator does with it

- Keep the responses in one private sheet. Add two columns: "matched this month" and "last matched".
- Never share the sheet, export it, or post it anywhere. Never paste it into an AI chat.
- Once a year, email every helper: "Still happy to help? Reply to change anything or to stop." Remove anyone who asks, the same day.
- When a helper withdraws, delete their row; do not just hide it.
