# Ask a member in your field: the community layer

For a church, campus ministry, alumni group, or any community where working adults and students already know and trust each other.

## The gap this fills

Job apps, trackers, and resume tools are everywhere, and most have free plans. What students rarely have is the one thing that helps most: **a person who does the work they want, who will give them 20 minutes.** Referrals from someone inside are one of the strongest known routes to a first job.

Many communities already have both sides. Working members post openings at their own companies. Students quietly struggle. What is usually missing is the link between them, and a way to ask that does not feel like begging in public ("post your resume here" is a hard ask for a 21-year-old).

This folder is that link. It needs no app, no platform, and no new software: two short forms, one coordinator, and the prompts in this kit.

## How it works

1. **Members opt in as helpers** with the helper form (`helper-opt-in.md`): their field, what they can offer (a 20-minute call, a resume look, a mock interview, a referral when there is a real fit), how many asks a month, and evenings or weekends. They can withdraw any time.
2. **Students ask privately** with the request form (`student-request.md`): what they study, what they are looking for, what would help. Nothing is posted publicly.
3. **One coordinator matches** each request to one helper by field, and sends a short introduction to both (`coordinator-guide.md`). The helper list is seen only by the coordinators. It is never a public directory.
4. **The student uses the kit** to make the most of it: the fit check (prompt 2) before any referral ask, the warm ask (prompt 4), and mock interview prep (prompt 5).
5. **A job club** (`job-club.md`) keeps students going between calls: 4 to 6 evenings, small group, one shared sheet, one goal a week each.

## What it costs

- One coordinator, about 30 minutes a week for every 10 requests.
- Two free forms (Google Forms, Microsoft Forms, or paper) and one private sheet.
- A job club needs one facilitator per group of 4 to 6 students.

## Principles

- **Opt in, and opt out any time.** Nobody is listed who did not ask to be.
- **Private by default.** The helper list and the requests are seen only by the coordinators. Counts can be shared ("12 students matched this term"); names cannot.
- **A member in your field, not "the church will get you a job".** What helps is a professional tie in the student's own field. The community makes the introduction; the student does the work.
- **Help is a gift.** No helper owes anyone a referral. A referral is offered only when the student's fit check passes and the helper is comfortable vouching for them.
- **Evenings and weekends.** Helpers have day jobs. So do most coordinators.
- **Honest on both sides.** Students use only true facts about themselves. Helpers give honest feedback, kindly.
- **No scraping.** Nothing in this kit reads your community's directory, member app, email, or drive. Members tell you about themselves through the form, and that is the only data there is.

## Files

- `helper-opt-in.md`: the helper form text and what the coordinator does with it.
- `student-request.md`: the student request form text.
- `coordinator-guide.md`: how to match, the introduction email, the monthly check.
- `job-club.md` and `job-club-sheet.csv`: running a 4 to 6 evening job club.
- `calling-and-work.md`: a blank page for your community's leaders to fill, if your community wants to talk about work and calling.
