# Coordinator guide

One or two coordinators run the match. About 30 minutes a week for every 10 requests.

## Each week

1. Open the request sheet. For each new request, reply to the student within a week, even if there is no match yet: "Thanks, I'm looking for the right person."
2. Find a helper whose field fits and who is under their monthly limit. Prefer someone who offered what the student asked for. Same campus group is a nice tie-breaker, not a rule.
3. Ask the helper first, by email, with only the student's first name, year, field, and ask: "Would you be open to a 20-minute call with a senior studying economics who wants to get into data analysis? No pressure either way." This double check is what keeps helpers happy to stay on the list.
4. On a yes, send the introduction below to both. Then step out; they take it from there.
5. Log the match: date, student, helper, what was asked. Add one to the helper's "matched this month".

If there is no helper in the field, tell the student honestly, offer the closest field, and point them to the kit and the job club.

## The introduction email

```text
Subject: Introduction: [student first name] and [helper first name]

Hi [helper first name] and [student first name],

[Helper first name], thank you for offering to help. [Student first name] is a [year] studying [field] and is looking for [kind of work]. They asked for [the ask].

[Student first name], [helper first name] works in [field] and offered evenings or weekends. Please reply with 2 or 3 times that work for you, and keep it to about 20 minutes.

I'll step out from here. Thank you both.

[Coordinator name]
```

## Referrals

- A referral is the helper's choice, always. The coordinator never asks a helper to refer anyone.
- Before a student asks for a referral, they run the fit check (prompt 2) on the post and share the result. If it does not say APPLY, the ask becomes a call instead.

## Once a month

- Send each student matched last month one line: "How did it go? Anything else that would help?"
- Count, do not name: requests, matches, calls that happened, and (if students tell you) interviews and offers. Share only the counts with your leaders.
- Thank the helpers who gave time, by name, privately.

## Privacy

- The helper sheet and the request sheet stay in your community's account, shared only with coordinators.
- Never paste either sheet, or anyone's answers, into an AI chat.
- Delete requests a year after the last contact.
