# Calling and work

This page is left blank on purpose.

Many communities, churches especially, want to help students think about work as more than a paycheck: what they are made for, how a job fits a life of service, how to choose when two paths are both good. That teaching should come from your community's own leaders and tradition, not from a toolkit or an AI chat.

**For leaders:** replace this page with your own material. A good page here is short: a few paragraphs, a few questions a student can sit with, and who to talk to. Then a job club can spend part of one evening on it.

Suggested questions to start from (edit freely):

- What work have I done, paid or not, that left me with more energy than it took?
- Who do I want to serve, and what problems do I want to be near?
- What would I do in my first job if I knew it was not my last?

**Not for AI chats.** The prompts in this kit are for practical steps. Questions of calling belong with people who know you.
