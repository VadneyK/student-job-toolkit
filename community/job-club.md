# Running a job club

A job search alone is slow and discouraging. A small group on a set evening makes it steadier. The groups that help most do three things together: they teach a skill, each person sets a goal, and people support each other. Support alone is not enough. In one careful trial, writing a job-search plan once helped more than a peer group added on top of it. So this club is led by a facilitator, starts with a written plan, and teaches something every week.

## The shape

- 4 to 6 students and one facilitator (a working member, or a recent grad who has been through it).
- 4 to 6 evenings, once a week, about 90 minutes. Friday evening with dinner works well; so does a weeknight.
- One shared sheet: `job-club-sheet.csv`. Import it into Google Sheets or Excel. One row per person per week.
- Everyone uses this kit, so everyone speaks the same language: profile card, fit check, Unknown, apply, skip.

## Each evening (90 minutes)

1. **Dinner and check-in (20 minutes).** Each person reads their row: last week's goal, what happened, asks made. Celebrate replies, calls, and interviews.
2. **Skill segment (20 minutes).** The facilitator teaches the week's skill (below), with one real example.
3. **Work time (30 minutes).** Laptops out. Everyone does the week's skill on their own search: real applications, a warm ask, interview practice. The facilitator walks around and helps.
4. **Goals (10 minutes).** Each person writes next week's goal in the sheet: applications to roles that pass the fit check, and one ask to a person.
5. **Close (10 minutes).** Share one thing you are thankful for, and pray for each other if that is your community's way.

## The weeks

1. **Your plan.** Everyone writes a one-page job-search plan, once, and keeps it: target roles (from prompt 1), where you will look, hours a week you will give, 3 kinds of people you will ask for help, and what you will do in the first two weeks. Then the profile card.
2. **Resume lines from what you have done.** Everyone logs 3 things in the kit page's Experience tab (church service, campus ministry, tutoring, part-time work) and reads the resume lines it makes. Swap with a partner: would you believe each line?
3. **Fit check and scam check.** Prompt 2 on real posts, out loud as a group; people disagree most on level and eligibility, and that is where everyone learns. Prompt 3 on a sample scam message.
4. **Asking for help.** Warm asks (prompt 4). The facilitator connects each person with one helper through the coordinator.
5. **Interviews.** Practice in pairs with prompt 5, or with a helper who joins for the evening.
6. **Proof of work and what next.** Plan one small project each (prompt 6), run the weekly review (prompt 7), and decide whether to keep meeting.

## The one-page plan (week 1)

```text
MY JOB SEARCH PLAN, written [date]
Target roles (up to 3):
Where I will look (sites, career fairs, people):
Hours a week I will give:
3 kinds of people I will ask for help (a field, not a name):
First two weeks, step by step:
How I will know it is working (for example: one call or interview a week by week 4):
```

## Ground rules

- What is said in the club stays in the club.
- Be honest and kind with each other's fit checks and resume lines.
- Share leads that do not fit you but might fit someone here.
- Only vouch for a friend for a role their fit check passes.
