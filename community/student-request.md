# Student request form

Copy this into a free form tool owned by your community's account. Only the coordinators see the answers. This replaces "post your resume publicly": a student can ask without anyone else knowing.

## Form text

**Title:** Talk to someone in your field

**Description:**
Looking for an internship or your first job? Tell us what you are studying and what would help, and a coordinator will try to connect you with a working member in your field, usually within two weeks. This is private: only the coordinators see it. There is no cost and no catch. People here are glad to help.

**Questions:**

1. Your name (required)
2. Email (required)
3. School and year, or "graduated [month year]" (required)
4. What you are studying (required)
5. The kind of work you want (required, short answer. "Not sure yet" is a fine answer.)
6. What would help most right now? (required, pick up to 2):
   - A 20-minute call with someone who does this work
   - Someone to look at my resume
   - A practice interview
   - Advice on a specific job post I'm applying to
   - I'm not sure, I just feel stuck
7. City, or remote (optional)
8. Your campus group, if any (optional)
9. Anything else (optional)
10. Checkbox (required): "I understand a coordinator will share my name, email, and answers 3 to 6 with one helper when introducing us, and no one else."

## After it is sent

The student sees: "Thank you. A coordinator will email you within a week. While you wait, you can start the free kit: [LINK TO THIS KIT]. Prompt 1 builds your profile card in 5 minutes."
